   files hold population + child poverty only — NO income at district grain —

   as such everywhere it is used.

   authorization effect concentrates in AFFLUENT districts — low-child-poverty
   τ=+0.184 (z=3.01) vs high +0.099 (n.s.) — matching the county split (rich
   +0.178 vs poor +0.119). Two grains, one gradient.

   without checking; the RD split above is within-matched and internally valid.
