## Interpretation
1. **The authorization effect is not partisan — an informative null.** The Dem-share
   split is flat (+0.121 vs +0.111) and the terciles show no monotone gradient
   (+0.163 R / +0.072 mid / +0.120 D). Combined with the freeholder result, the
   moderator profile is INSTITUTIONAL and PROPRIETARY, not ideological: what
   predicts binding is who owns property and how stable the electorate is, not
   which party it votes for. This defuses a "red-state phenomenon" objection to
   the pooled estimate in one table.
2. **Bond bank is uninformative within the RD frame** — the split reduces to CA
   vs the rest (a state grouping), and the τ difference (+0.166 vs +0.131) is
   within noise. The variable's proper use is the NATIONAL track (C1/C2), where
   all 50 states vary; carried there, not here.
