## Interpretation
1. **The null replicates with the better measures, within wide bands.** Every
   subgroup — precinct-based city Dem share (median split) and mayor party at
   the vote — is insignificant, with no Dem/Rep asymmetry beyond noise
   (SEs 0.08–0.35). The county-grain conclusion stands where the superior
   city-footprint measure exists.
2. **The deeper reason is D4's:** large general-purpose cities are the
   exit-rich class whose issuance shows no authorization discontinuity to
   moderate in the first place (any-purpose pooled τ's here hover near zero on
   both sides of every split). Partisanship cannot moderate an effect that the
   chargeable exit has already dissolved.
3. Power stated plainly: 1,932 rd_sample municipal measures fall in the 577-city
   panel (862 bond_go; 178 within ±10pp) — this is a consistency check, not an
   independent test. The definitive partisanship evidence remains the full-frame
   county-grain null (D5_EXTERNAL_RESULTS.md).
