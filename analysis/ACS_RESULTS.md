## Interpretation — the freeholder test (H3 headline)
1. **Voter authorization binds where property owners dominate the consenting
   public.** At proper grain, high-homeownership jurisdictions show τ=+0.182
   (z=3.47) vs +0.056 (n.s.) below the median; the gradient survives at full
   coverage (+0.192 z=4.43 vs +0.090). The "de facto freeholder franchise"
   claim now has direct empirical support.
2. **It is ownership, not income:** the income split is flat at proper grain
   (+0.129 vs +0.122) — the active margin is property-holding, not affluence.
   (The earlier child-poverty/income gradients partly proxied ownership.)
3. **Stability of the consenting public is the common thread:** 65+ (+0.194 vs
   +0.036) and racial-ethnic HOMOGENEITY (+0.232 vs −0.018 at proper grain)
   also carry the effect — older, homogeneous, property-owning electorates issue
   on their authorizations; diverse/renter electorates' refusals get unwound.
   NOTE the fractionalization sign is OPPOSITE the naive assembly-cost reading
   of H3 — the pattern favors a "stable propertied public" interpretation over
   a pure transaction-cost one; the theory session should adjudicate.
4. Grain: place for municipals (8,259 rows), SD for schools (7,020), county
   fallback elsewhere (specials by design). Vintage 2010 for votes ≤2015, 2019
   after (2016–19 votes overlap the window — moderator-safe, flagged for
   balance use).
